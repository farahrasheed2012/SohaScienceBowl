# Reading pace — 1-hour blocks

## Summer totals

| | Old (30 min) | New (1 hr) |
|---|-------------|------------|
| Science per weekday | 30 min | **60 min** |
| Algebra per weekday | 30 min (Mon–Thu) | **60 min (Mon–Fri)** |
| Science hours / week | 2.5 hr | **5 hr** |
| Study hours / week | 4 hr (Mon–Thu) | **10 hr** |

**50 science blocks** × 1 hr = **50 hours** of science (was 25). That is enough to cover the full Pass 1–3 curriculum when each block targets **one chapter** (or one half of a former two-chapter day).

## One chapter per hour?

- **Most textbook chapters:** yes, ~45–60 min of careful reading fits in one block, plus app Focus / Know cold / toss-ups.
- **Former combo days** (e.g. `Ch 7 + Ch 8`): split across **two same-subject days in the same week** (biology Tue + Fri; chemistry Mon + Thu).
- **Physics (Wed only):** multi-chapter ranges split across **consecutive Wednesdays** (e.g. Newton's Ch 2 → Ch 3 → wrap-up in Pass 2).
- **Topics deferred to Pass 2:** energy (Expl Ch 6), electricity (Ch 13), microbes/immunity/plants — reviewed in weeks 5–8 with DOE-heavy blocks.

## Still cover everything?

Yes. The calendar keeps **50 science slots** and **50 algebra slots**. Pass 2 revisits the same topics with Tro/CB/DOE; Pass 3 is flash-card review. Splitting combos across days does **not** remove topics — it spreads them so Soha can finish reading without rushing.
